About

Detroit Shows is a curated calendar of live music events in the Detroit area, with a focus on experimental, improvisational, and boldly adventurous performances.

The site pulls show data from various venues and services, including Michigan Happenings, Trinosophes, Moondog Cafe, Cliff Bell's,and The Blue Llama. Events are also added manually - If you'd like to see your event on the site, please reach out via the contact form.

You can also subscribe to the weekly newsletter to get the upcoming week's shows delivered to your inbox every Sunday evening.

It's a labor of love, and your support helps keep it going. If you'd like to contribute, you can donate $$$ here.
